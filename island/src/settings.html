WinIsland

Welcome to WinIsland

A Dynamic Island for Windows. Notifications, music and video, timers, battery, volume and more appear in the island at the top of your screen. Hover over the island to see details. Click it to expand it.

Three quick steps

1
Let WinIsland read notifications

Windows asks once. You can also turn on Notification access for WinIsland in Privacy & security.

2
Hide Windows’ own pop-ups

Turn on Do not disturb in Windows, or turn off Show notification banners for each app. Windows still collects your notifications, so WinIsland still shows them. You just won’t see the same pop-up twice.

3
Try it

Send a test notification, or preview a live activity. To control music and video, just play something: Spotify, YouTube in any browser, YouTube Music, Media Player and more.

Tips

Hover the island to peek at what’s happening. Click it to expand it.

In the expanded island, the tabs along the bottom switch between Now playing, Timers, Notifications and Home.

Drag the progress bar to move through a song or video. Scroll over it to jump 5 seconds.

Right-click the island to open these settings. The tray icon has a timer menu and Do not disturb.
